🏦 Population Group-Wise Banking Deposits & Financial Inclusion Analysis (2019–2026)
   
An end-to-end data analytics and statistical modeling project evaluating spatial deposit patterns, financial inclusion dynamics, branch network distribution, and capital mobilization efficiency across Indian districts and population groups (2019–2026).


📖 Project Overview

This repository contains a full-lifecycle empirical analysis of district-level and population group-wise banking deposit datasets in India spanning 2019 to 2026.

The primary objective is to evaluate financial inclusion progress, branch infrastructure yield, and capital concentration across four demographic tiers: Rural, Semi-Urban, Urban, and Metropolitan. The analysis systematically applies data cleaning, targeted missing-value imputation, feature engineering, descriptive/inferential statistics, and univariate/bivariate/multivariate visualizations.

📊 Dataset Information

Source: Open Data Portals / Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Open Data
Geographic Coverage: All Indian States, Union Territories, and Districts
Time Horizon: 2019 to 2026
Domain: Banking, Finance & Macro-Economics
Record Count: ~14,968 empirical observations

🎯 Key Project Objectives

Infrastructure & Penetration: Examine bank office distribution and account density per branch.
Longitudinal Growth: Track multi-year deposit growth trajectories across 2019–2026.
Spatial Capital Disparity: Quantify capital concentration differences between metropolitan hubs and rural/semi-urban tiers.
Statistical Rigor: Compute Central Tendency, Variance, Standard Deviation, Skewness, and Kurtosis to handle long-tailed financial data appropriately.
Data-Driven Policy Guidance: Formulate actionable recommendations for financial institutions and policymakers.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

Programming Language: Python 3.10+
Data Wrangling & Analysis: NumPy, Pandas
Data Visualization: Matplotlib, Seaborn
Environment: Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook / VS Code

💡 Key Findings & Business Insights

Two-Tier Banking Reality: Rural and Semi-Urban areas drive massive volume-based financial inclusion (accounts grew ~32% from 2019 to 2026), whereas Metropolitan nodes control over 70% of total deposit capital.

Capital Density: Metropolitan branches mobilize over 60 times more deposit volume per office compared to rural branch infrastructure.

Infrastructure Alignment: Physical branch presence exhibits a strong positive correlation (r>0.85) with aggregate deposit collection capacity.

Strategic Recommendations:

Targeted Rural Wealth Products: Introduce specialized term-deposit products tailored to rural agrarian savings to raise low per-account deposit density.

Hybrid Rural Branch Expansion: Deploy low-cost micro-branches and Banking Correspondents (BCs) in semi-urban corridors to capture emerging retail capital efficiently.
